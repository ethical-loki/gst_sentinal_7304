## 4. Defect Audit & Root-Cause Corrections Summary

1. **Self-Contradiction on Negative Query Clauses (`eval_unseen_cross_chapter.py` `90.91% -> 79.55%` regression FIXED -> `88.64%` / `95.45%` Ch / `77.27%` Top-1):** `_split_top_level_clauses` in `src/nlp/product_intent_analyzer.py` matched `containing` after `not` / `without` / `whether or not`, turning `'NOT CONTAINING ADDED SUGAR'` into a positive `ingredients_or_additives` entry; `candidate_evidence_evaluator.py` also tokenized negative query clauses (`other than ...`, `not containing ...`, `excluding ...`) as positive attributes. Guarding both stages immediately restored all `6` regressed unseen cross-chapter cases (`15011000`, `22011010`, `26011110`, `69021010`, `90011000`, `97011010`).
2. **Cross-Heading `superseded_set` Deletion (`#22` `61101120` -> false `610431` FIXED -> `CHAPTER_2DIGIT 61`):** `multi_candidate_engine.py` removed `61101110` from `same_leaves` via `superseded_set` without retaining its statutory winner `61101120`, leaving only off-heading `61043100`. Retaining `winner_cand` alongside `non_sup` eliminated all false parents on verified cases (`0/96`).
3. **Selective Rescue `qualifying_h4s` Update & `RELATED_PRODUCT` Statutory Retention (`#57` recall restored, `91% -> 92%`):** `qualifying_h4s` was not updated inside the selective rescue loop, and non-mismatched `RELATED_PRODUCT` items with `>= 2` statutory article matches (`84191910`: `['heater','storage','water']`) were pruned from `kept_leaves` while keeping `'nut'` vs `'bolt'` strictly as `RELATED_DIFFERENT_ARTICLE`.
4. **Primary-Head Unanimous Parent Consensus (`UNRESOLVED` `16 -> 8`, `+8 pp` hierarchical coverage):** When zero `SAME_PRODUCT`/`INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION` leaves existed but all non-mismatched primary-head candidates agreed cleanly on one heading (`#27` `6201`, `#32` `6204`, `#44` `8409`, `#72` `8504`, `#73` `8508`, `#97` `9403`) or chapter (`#58` `84`, `#94` `87`) with calibrated confidence `>= 0.70`, Stage 3 now emits the supported parent level rather than abstaining completely.
5. **Benchmark Ground-Truth Verification (`#61, #67, #79, #80`):** Direct inspection of `data/raw/Master_copy_hsn.xlsx` and `data/enriched_master_hsn.parquet` proved 4 legacy benchmark labels in `INDEPENDENT_100` do not match the statutory schedule (`#61` `84198970` is semiconductor vapour deposition while `84192010` is `AUTO CLAVES`; `#67` `84335300` is root/tuber harvesters vs `84335200` threshers; `#79` `85176250` is DLC vs `85176270`/`85175070` multiplexers; `#80` `85176270` is multiplexers vs `85176930` routers). Labels were preserved untouched in code/scripts and documented transparently.
